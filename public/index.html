OUR NEXT CHAPTER · MASJID AT-TOWFIQ

Our new masjid project

Alhamdulillah, a new home for our growing community is taking shape. The masjid has secured a new property and arrangements are now progressing for its next chapter.

Property secured · Arrangements in progress

A place to serve generations to come.

This is a significant milestone for At-Towfiiq Islamic and Cultural Charity. Our community's generosity and du’as have helped bring the vision of a new masjid within reach.

As arrangements continue for the property, donations remain important to support the project and the work ahead. Whether you can give, share the appeal or make du’a, your support is valued.

New property: Temperance Street, Bolton BL3 5BX

This is the new project location, not a replacement for the current prayer and visiting address at 24 Punch Street. Please use the current masjid address until an official move is announced.
