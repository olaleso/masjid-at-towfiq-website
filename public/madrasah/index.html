01 OUR MADRASAH

Learning that reaches beyond the classroom.

At Madrasah At-Towfiq, education is about more than completing a lesson. We want learners to build a sound foundation in recitation, develop understanding and carry the values they learn into their everyday lives.

Our teaching areas bring Qur’an, Qaaidah and Islamic Studies together, with families and teachers working in partnership to support steady progress.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions from families

We can help with details that depend on class placement and the current timetable.

How do I enquire about admission? +

Select “Start an application” above to complete the online form. You do not need a portal login to apply. If online admissions are unavailable, contact the masjid team.

What ages and levels do you teach? +

Teaching covers Qaaidah, Qur’an and Islamic Studies. Please contact the team for current age groups, levels and available places; we do not publish unconfirmed age limits.

When are classes held? +

Please ask the madrasah team for the current lesson timetable, holiday arrangements and any fees. These details have not yet been published on this website.

How does the family portal work? +

Eligible guardians receive account access following the admissions process. The portal keeps homework, family updates and student information in a secure environment.
