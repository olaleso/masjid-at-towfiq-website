Before you apply

Please have your child's name, date of birth, address and your contact details ready.

  • Submit one form for each child.
  • There is no fee to submit this application.
  • Our admissions team will review the details and contact you about next steps.
  • Do not send sensitive documents unless requested through an approved secure process.
